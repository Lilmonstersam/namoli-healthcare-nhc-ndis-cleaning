Specialist Cleaning for Care and Respite Settings

Care and respite settings face infection risks that ordinary commercial premises do not. Vulnerable residents and participants, shared bathrooms, high-touch surfaces and constant foot traffic require more specialised protocols than standard commercial cleaning provides. Our protocols reflect the way your facilities operate.

Residential Care Facilities

Resident rooms, ensuites, dining rooms and communal areas cleaned according to documented healthcare protocols, with outbreak response procedures ready when needed. In dementia and memory support areas, our teams follow consistent routines planned around residents' schedules.

NDIS Respite and Day Centres

High-turnover respite rooms and day programme spaces cleaned and prepared between participants, with enhanced cleans between stays so every arrival meets the same standard.

Documentation That Stands Up to Audit

Quality managers need clear evidence of cleaning performance. Namoli provides documented cleaning programmes that support accreditation reviews, quality audits and stakeholder enquiries, whether you operate one centre or an entire portfolio.

  • Screened, Trained Teams

    Cleaning personnel undergo background screening, including NDIS Worker Screening Checks and Police Checks as required by facility type, and hold site-appropriate infection control training.

  • Standards-Aligned Protocols

    Cleaning programmes developed with reference to applicable Aged Care Quality Standards and, for NDIS services, the NDIS Practice Standards.

  • Consistent Standards Across Every Site

    One set of protocols, one account manager and one reporting format across your portfolio, with audit results consolidated at group level so you can compare centres and identify performance gaps early.

  • Audit-Ready Documentation

    Cleaning schedules, Safety Data Sheets, training and screening records, audit results and corrective action logs are maintained and available for review.

  • Verified, Not Assumed

    ATP testing and 3M protein pens measure what the eye cannot see, giving your quality team objective evidence of cleaning performance.
